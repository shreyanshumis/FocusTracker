# FocusTracker

**Choose what gets your attention.**

FocusTracker is a simple website blocker for Windows that helps you stay focused while studying or working. Choose the websites that distract you, decide when to block them, and take a break when you need one.

## Your websites. Your choice.

Start with an empty list and add only the websites you want to block. Enter a name like `reddit.com` or paste a full link like `https://www.reddit.com/`. FocusTracker treats both as the same website, rather than blocking just one page.

Add or remove websites whenever you like. Nothing is selected for you.

## Focus on your schedule

- **Block now:** Keep your chosen websites blocked until you unblock them or switch modes.
- **Focus timers:** Set a countdown for a focused session or a short break.
- **Weekly schedules:** Block distractions automatically during the hours you choose.
- **Stay in control:** Unblock websites, remove individual entries, or clear your entire list.

FocusTracker stays in the system tray while you work. Keep it running for timers and schedules to take effect.

## A pause before YouTube

When you add YouTube to your list, FocusTracker asks:

> Are you sure it's about studies?

Choose to continue for studying or return to your work. The prompt is a reminder; it does not decide whether a video is educational.

## Simple and private

No account, subscription, or cloud connection is needed. Your settings stay on your PC, and FocusTracker does not collect browsing history. A clean blue interface, smooth transitions, and an optional Reduce motion setting keep the app easy to use.

## Works on Windows

FocusTracker supports **Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit)** and installs as a desktop app. Administrator permission is required for website blocking.

The included **Chrome and Edge browser companion** is required for YouTube prompts, blocking subdomains, and stopping already-open website tabs. Set it up in each browser you use. Without it, basic website blocking may require a browser restart and can be bypassed by some network settings.

FocusTracker blocks websites, including browser games. It does not block installed applications.

## Easy to install. Easy to remove.

Run the Windows installer, approve the Windows permission prompt, and click **Install**. Leave **Launch FocusTracker** selected and click **Finish** to open the app. A desktop shortcut is selected by default, and the app is also available from Start. No Python installation or terminal commands are needed.

To remove it, choose **FocusTracker > Uninstall** in Windows Settings. The uninstaller asks the running app to close safely and removes its website-blocking rules before deleting the app. Saved preferences are kept if you reinstall. If you added the browser companion, remove it from your browser separately.
